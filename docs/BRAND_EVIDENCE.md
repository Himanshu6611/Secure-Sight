# Brand evidence and explanations

The bounded evidence graph links the actual final page, registrable domain, observed registry brands and dated registration/first-observation/change events. Page-brand edges retain field provenance and the STATIC_PAGE independence group. Repeated title/headings/body/logo claims are not independent reputation sources.

Phase 9 observations use source phase_9 and known evidence IDs. Phase 6 validates version, scalar ranges, binary integer types, corroboration consistency, actual password counts, registry identities and reason IDs. It adapts a single existing brand.mismatch signal; new similarity/history/email observations have zero extra risk contribution. No age or history bonus is added on top of existing Phase 3 age policy.

Phase 7 permits phase_9 provenance for the existing brand signal and translates bounded contextual observations from trusted fixed descriptions. It adds HISTORICAL category and brand_reasons; raw page instructions/provider strings cannot become explanation text. Contextual reasons are separate from scored top reasons, and output-size limits also cover brand_reasons.

Optional existing scan API email_context allows only sender_domain, reply_to_domain and a registry claimed_brand ID. Domains are normalized and validated; mailbox addresses, headers, credentials and arbitrary objects are rejected. Correlation compares supplied domains with the detected page brand and final analyzed destination. This is PARTIAL: sender authentication, full email parsing/link extraction and automatic crawling of all email URLs are not implemented. Differences are contextual, unscored and do not prove spoofing.

Evidence confidence remains provisional. Official matches, zero mismatch and unavailable history do not guarantee authenticity or safety.
